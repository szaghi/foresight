The data modules are generated by `scripts/gen_fonts.py` from the font files: a subset of the glyphs (Latin, Greek,
punctuation and math symbols), their advance widths, kerning pairs and outlines, converted to Fortran arrays. Under the
OFL this is a Modified Version: it keeps the OFL, it is not sold by itself, and it is not presented under the Reserved
Font Names (Liberation, Arimo, Tinos, Cousine): foresight calls its faces `sans` and `serif`. Documents foresight
writes with these fonts (SVG, PDF) are not covered by the font licenses (OFL section 5).

The generator itself (`scripts/gen_fonts.py`) and the lookup module `src/lib/foresight_fontdata.F90` are foresight's
code.
