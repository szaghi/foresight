# D4. Colored and lit surfaces

**Goal:** the surface as colored faces, lit, so its shape reads at a glance.

## Why it works

- `with pm3d` fills each grid cell with the palette color of its height (the mean of its corners by default, `set
  pm3d corners2color` for others).
- `set pm3d depthorder` draws the cells from the back to the front, so a nearer cell hides a farther one: the
  painter's algorithm, gnuplot's.
- `lighting primary 0.5 specular 0.3` shades each cell by its angle to a light from the viewer, with highlights: the
  slopes and the dips become visible without contour lines.

## Try this

- `set pm3d at b`: the colors on the base plane under a mesh, the map and the surface together.
- `splot ... with pm3d fc 'light-blue'` and lighting: a single color, the shape from the light alone.
- `set pm3d border lc 'black' lw 0.3`: the grid lines over the faces.

## Pitfalls

- Depth sorting is per cell: surfaces that cut through each other show the cut in steps of a cell.
- Lighting darkens the colors: a reader cannot read values off a lit surface, keep the color box for the values or
  add contours.
